Article 11 — Technical Documentation (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

1. The technical documentation of a high-risk AI system shall be drawn up before that system is placed on the market or put into service and shall be kept up-to-date. The technical documentation shall be drawn up in such a way as to demonstrate that the high-risk AI system complies with the requirements laid down in this Regulation and to provide national competent authorities and notified bodies with all the necessary information to assess the compliance of the AI system with those requirements.

2. The technical documentation shall contain, at a minimum, the following information, as applicable to the high-risk AI system concerned:
   (a) a general description of the AI system including its intended purpose, the name and contact details of the provider and, where applicable, its authorised representative;
   (b) a detailed description of the system architecture, including the algorithms, data flows, and processing operations;
   (c) a description of the data sets used for training, validation and testing, including information on data sources, data collection processes, and data governance practices;
   (d) a description of the human oversight measures implemented in accordance with Article 14;
   (e) a description of the accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity measures implemented in accordance with Article 15;
   (f) a description of the risk management system implemented in accordance with Article 9;
   (g) information on the performance of the AI system, including metrics for accuracy, robustness, and any limitations or known biases;
   (h) information on the conformity assessment procedure followed and the notified body involved, where applicable.

3. The technical documentation shall be kept up to date throughout the lifecycle of the high-risk AI system. Any significant modifications to the AI system shall be documented and the technical documentation shall be updated accordingly.

4. The technical documentation shall be made available to national competent authorities upon request, in an official Union language or in a language accepted by the competent authority.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 11. Technical documentation requirements for high-risk AI systems.
