Article 12 — Record-Keeping

1. High-risk AI systems shall be designed and developed with the technical capability to automatically record events (logs) while the high-risk AI system is operating. Those logging capabilities shall ensure a level of traceability of the functioning of the AI system throughout its lifecycle that is appropriate to the intended purpose of the system.

2. Logging capabilities shall enable, in particular:
   (a) the identification of situations that may result in the high-risk AI system presenting a risk within the meaning of Article 79(1) or in a substantial modification;
   (b) the facilitation of the post-market monitoring referred to in Article 72;
   (c) the monitoring of the operation of high-risk AI systems, in particular in the case of systems that continue to learn after being placed on the market or put into service.

3. For high-risk AI systems referred to in points 1(a) to 1(c) and 1(e) and (g) of Annex III (biometric identification, critical infrastructure, education, law enforcement), the logging capabilities shall provide, at a minimum:
   (a) recording of the period of each use of the system (start date and time and end date and time of each use);
   (b) the reference database against which input data has been checked;
   (c) the input data in respect of which the search has led to a match;
   (d) the identification of the natural persons involved in the verification of the results.

4. The logs referred to in this Article shall be kept for a period appropriate to the intended purpose of the high-risk AI system and, in any event, for no less than six months, unless provided otherwise in applicable Union or national law, in particular in the area of law enforcement.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 12.
